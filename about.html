What We Believe

The Holy Bible

We believe the entire Bible is the inspired Word of God and that men were moved by the Spirit of God to write the words of Scripture. It serves as our supreme guide for faith and life.

The Trinity

We believe in one God who eternally exists in three distinct persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. We believe Jesus Christ is the Son of God who revealed the Father's love to humanity.

Salvation & Grace

We believe that salvation is a gift received through grace by putting faith in Jesus Christ, whose sacrifice on the cross offers complete forgiveness, redemption, and eternal hope.
